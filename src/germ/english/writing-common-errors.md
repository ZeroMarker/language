# 英语常见语法错误纠正

## 主谓一致错误

| 错误 | 正确 | 说明 |
|------|------|------|
| The team are winning. | The team **is** winning. | 团队名称作单数（美式） |
| Everyone have their own opinion. | Everyone **has** **his/her/their** own opinion. | everyone 为单数 |
| The news are bad. | The news **is** bad. | news 为不可数名词 |
| Mathematics are difficult. | Mathematics **is** difficult. | 学科名称作单数 |
| Neither of them are correct. | Neither of them **is** correct. | neither 为单数 |

## 时态错误

| 错误 | 正确 | 说明 |
|------|------|------|
| I am knowing the answer. | I **know** the answer. | know 是状态动词，不用进行时 |
| He has went home. | He **has gone** home. | 完成时用过去分词 gone |
| I am living here since 2020. | I **have lived** here since 2020. | since 搭配完成时 |
| She suggested me to go. | She **suggested that I (should) go**. | suggest 后接 that 从句 |
| When I arrived, he left. | When I arrived, he **had left**. | 先发生的动作用过去完成时 |

## 冠词错误

| 错误 | 正确 | 说明 |
|------|------|------|
| I like the music. (泛指) | I like **music**. | 泛指不加 the |
| He is teacher. | He is **a** teacher. | 单数可数名词前需冠词 |
| I go to the school every day. (指上学) | I go to **school** every day. | go to school 表"上学" |
| a honest man | **an** honest man | h 不发音，元音音素开头 |

## 介词错误

| 错误 | 正确 | 说明 |
|------|------|------|
| discuss about the problem | **discuss** the problem | discuss 是及物动词 |
| enter into the room | **enter** the room | enter 是及物动词 |
| depend of | depend **on** | 固定搭配 |
| married with her | married **to** her | 固定搭配 |
| different than | different **from** | 英式标准搭配 |
| in Monday | **on** Monday | 星期前用 on |
| in the night | **at** night | night 前用 at |

## 代词错误

| 错误 | 正确 | 说明 |
|------|------|------|
| Me and Tom went there. | **Tom and I** went there. | 主格，且他人在前 |
| Between you and I. | Between you and **me**. | 介词后用宾格 |
| His book is more better. | His book is **better**. | 比较级不叠用 more |
| Who did you see? | **Whom** did you see? | 宾格（正式用法） |

## 句子结构错误

### 逗号拼接（Comma Splice）

| 错误 | 正确 |
|------|------|
| I like coffee, she likes tea. | I like coffee**;** she likes tea. |
| | I like coffee**.** She likes tea. |
| | I like coffee**, and** she likes tea. |
| | I like coffee**, while** she likes tea. |

### 悬垂修饰语（Dangling Modifier）

| 错误 | 正确 |
|------|------|
| Walking down the street, the trees were beautiful. | Walking down the street, **I** found the trees beautiful. |
| After reading the book, it was interesting. | After reading the book, **I** found it interesting. |

### 残句（Fragment）

| 错误 | 正确 |
|------|------|
| Because I was tired. | I stayed home **because** I was tired. |
| Running in the park. | **He was** running in the park. |

## 常见拼写错误

| 错误 | 正确 |
|------|------|
| recieve | rec**ei**ve |
| seperate | sep**a**rate |
| occured | occur**r**ed |
| untill | unti**l** |
| definately | defin**i**tely |
| goverment | govern**m**ent |
| neccessary | nece**ss**ary |
| accomodate | accomm**o**date |
| wierd | w**ei**rd |
| beleive | bel**ie**ve |

> 记忆技巧：
> - i before e, except after c: receive, believe
> - a rat in separate
> - one collar, one c (necessary: one c, two s)

## 用词混淆

| 混淆 | 区别 |
|------|------|
| its / it's | its = 它的；it's = it is |
| their / there / they're | their = 他们的；there = 那里；they're = they are |
| your / you're | your = 你的；you're = you are |
| affect / effect | affect = 动词；effect = 名词 |
| then / than | then = 然后；than = 比较 |
| who / whom | who = 主格；whom = 宾格 |
