# 英语常用谚语与习语

## 常见谚语（Proverbs）

| 英语 | 中文含义 |
|------|----------|
| Actions speak louder than words. | 行动胜于言语。 |
| All that glitters is not gold. | 闪光的不一定是金子。 |
| Better late than never. | 迟到总比不到好。 |
| Don't judge a book by its cover. | 不要以貌取人。 |
| Every cloud has a silver lining. | 塞翁失马，焉知非福。 |
| Practice makes perfect. | 熟能生巧。 |
| Rome wasn't built in a day. | 罗马不是一天建成的。（冰冻三尺非一日之寒） |
| The early bird catches the worm. | 早起的鸟儿有虫吃。 |
| Time is money. | 时间就是金钱。 |
| When in Rome, do as the Romans do. | 入乡随俗。 |
| Where there's a will, there's a way. | 有志者事竟成。 |
| You can't have your cake and eat it too. | 鱼与熊掌不可兼得。 |

## 常见习语（Idioms）

### 与身体相关的习语

| 习语 | 含义 | 示例 |
|------|------|------|
| break a leg | 祝好运 | I heard you have an interview — break a leg! |
| cost an arm and a leg | 非常昂贵 | That car cost an arm and a leg. |
| hit the nail on the head | 一针见血 | You hit the nail on the head with that comment. |
| keep an eye on | 留意、照看 | Keep an eye on the baby for me. |
| play it by ear | 随机应变 | Let's just play it by ear. |
| cold feet | 临阵退缩 | He got cold feet before the wedding. |
| get something off one's chest | 一吐为快 | I need to get this off my chest. |

### 与动物相关的习语

| 习语 | 含义 | 示例 |
|------|------|------|
| let the cat out of the bag | 泄露秘密 | She let the cat out of the bag about the party. |
| raining cats and dogs | 倾盆大雨 | It's raining cats and dogs outside. |
| the elephant in the room | 显而易见却被忽视的问题 | Let's address the elephant in the room. |
| hold your horses | 等一等、别急 | Hold your horses — we're not ready yet. |
| a piece of cake | 小菜一碟 | The exam was a piece of cake. |
| when pigs fly | 永远不可能 | He'll apologize when pigs fly. |

### 与颜色相关的习语

| 习语 | 含义 | 示例 |
|------|------|------|
| see red | 非常生气 | I saw red when I heard the news. |
| out of the blue | 突然地 | He called me out of the blue. |
| green with envy | 非常嫉妒 | She was green with envy. |
| white lie | 善意的谎言 | I told a white lie to spare her feelings. |
| in the black | 盈利 | The company is finally in the black. |
| in the red | 亏损 | We've been in the red for months. |

### 常用口语习语

| 习语 | 含义 | 示例 |
|------|------|------|
| bite the bullet | 咬紧牙关（硬着头皮做） | I had to bite the bullet and tell the truth. |
| burn the midnight oil | 开夜车 | I was burning the midnight oil for the exam. |
| hit the sack | 去睡觉 | I'm tired — I'm going to hit the sack. |
| on the same page | 意见一致 | Let's make sure we're on the same page. |
| under the weather | 身体不适 | I'm feeling under the weather today. |
| the ball is in your court | 轮到你了 | I've done my part; the ball is in your court now. |
| beat around the bush | 拐弯抹角 | Stop beating around the bush and say what you mean. |
| bite off more than you can chew | 贪多嚼不烂 | I bit off more than I could chew with this project. |

## 使用建议

- 习语的理解需要结合语境，字面意思往往与实际含义不同
- 习语多用于口语和非正式写作中
- 不同英语国家可能使用不同的习语
