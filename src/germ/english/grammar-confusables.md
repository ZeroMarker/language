# 英语易混词辨析

## 常见易混词对

### their / there / they're

| 词 | 词性 | 含义 | 示例 |
|----|------|------|------|
| their | 代词（所有格） | 他们的 | This is **their** house. |
| there | 副词 | 那里 | The book is over **there**. |
| they're | 缩写 | they are | **They're** coming tomorrow. |

### its / it's

| 词 | 词性 | 含义 | 示例 |
|----|------|------|------|
| its | 代词（所有格） | 它的 | The dog wagged **its** tail. |
| it's | 缩写 | it is / it has | **It's** raining. / **It's** been a long day. |

### your / you're

| 词 | 词性 | 含义 | 示例 |
|----|------|------|------|
| your | 所有格 | 你的 | Is this **your** book? |
| you're | 缩写 | you are | **You're** welcome. |

### affect / effect

| 词 | 词性 | 含义 | 示例 |
|----|------|------|------|
| affect | 动词 | 影响 | The weather **affects** my mood. |
| effect | 名词 | 效果、影响 | The **effect** was immediate. |

> 记忆技巧：**A**ffect = **A**ction（动词）；**E**ffect = **E**nd result（名词）

### then / than

| 词 | 词性 | 含义 | 示例 |
|----|------|------|------|
| then | 副词 | 然后、那时 | We ate, **then** left. |
| than | 连词 | 比（比较） | She is taller **than** me. |

### to / too / two

| 词 | 词性 | 含义 | 示例 |
|----|------|------|------|
| to | 介词/不定式标记 | 到、去 / to do | go **to** school / want **to** eat |
| too | 副词 | 也；太 | Me **too**. / **too** hot |
| two | 数词 | 二 | I have **two** cats. |

### accept / except

| 词 | 词性 | 含义 | 示例 |
|----|------|------|------|
| accept | 动词 | 接受 | I **accept** your offer. |
| except | 介词 | 除…之外 | Everyone came **except** Tom. |

### lose / loose

| 词 | 词性 | 含义 | 示例 |
|----|------|------|------|
| lose | 动词 | 丢失；输掉 | Don't **lose** your keys. |
| loose | 形容词 | 松的 | The screw is **loose**. |

### quiet / quite

| 词 | 词性 | 含义 | 示例 |
|----|------|------|------|
| quiet | 形容词 | 安静的 | Be **quiet**, please. |
| quite | 副词 | 相当、十分 | It's **quite** good. |

### lie / lay

| 词 | 词性 | 含义 | 过去式 |
|----|------|------|--------|
| lie | 不及物动词 | 躺；位于 | lay → lain |
| lay | 及物动词 | 放置；下蛋 | laid → laid |

> 易混点：lie 的过去式是 lay，与 lay 的原形相同。

### fewer / less

| 词 | 搭配 | 示例 |
|----|------|------|
| fewer | 可数名词复数 | **fewer** books, **fewer** people |
| less | 不可数名词 | **less** water, **less** time |

## 快速检测练习

判断下列句子中哪个词是正确的：

1. (Your / You're) going to love this.
2. The cat licked (it's / its) paws.
3. I have (less / fewer) friends now.
4. She is taller (then / than) her brother.
5. Everyone (accept / except) me agreed.
