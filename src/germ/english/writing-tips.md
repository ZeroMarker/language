# 英语写作技巧指导

## 段落结构

### 基本段落模式

一个标准段落包含：

1. **主题句（Topic Sentence）**：概括段落中心思想
2. **支撑句（Supporting Sentences）**：展开论证或提供细节
3. **结尾句（Concluding Sentence）**：总结或过渡

> **示例：**
> *Remote work has become increasingly popular in recent years.* Many companies have adopted flexible policies that allow employees to work from home. This arrangement offers benefits such as reduced commuting time and improved work-life balance. However, it also presents challenges like maintaining team communication. *As a result, hybrid work models are emerging as a practical compromise.*

### 段落展开方式

| 方式 | 适用场景 | 连接词 |
|------|----------|--------|
| 举例 | 支撑论点 | for example, for instance, such as |
| 对比 | 比较差异 | however, on the other hand, in contrast |
| 因果 | 解释原因/结果 | therefore, as a result, because |
| 时间顺序 | 叙事/流程 | first, then, finally, meanwhile |
| 递进 | 加强论述 | moreover, furthermore, in addition |

## 不同文体要点

### 议论文（Essay）

- 明确的论点（thesis statement）
- 每段一个分论点
- 充分的论据和例证
- 客观的语气

### 书信（Letter）

- **正式信件**：Dear Sir/Madam → Yours faithfully
- **半正式信件**：Dear Mr. Smith → Yours sincerely
- **非正式信件**：Dear John → Best wishes / Love

### 邮件（Email）

- 主题行简洁明确
- 开头问候得体
- 正文简短、分段清晰
- 结尾明确行动需求

## 常用句型模板

### 开头引入

- It is widely believed that...
- In recent years, there has been a growing concern about...
- With the development of..., ... has become increasingly important.
- There is no denying that...

### 表达观点

- In my opinion / From my perspective...
- I firmly believe that...
- It seems to me that...
- I am convinced that...

### 举例论证

- A good example of this is...
- This can be illustrated by...
- ... is a case in point.
- Take... for example.

### 总结收尾

- In conclusion, ...
- To sum up, ...
- All things considered, ...
- Taking everything into account, ...
