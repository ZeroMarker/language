# 英语介词专题

## 基本概念

介词（Preposition）用于表示名词/代词与其他词之间的关系，常见于时间、地点、方向、方式等语境。

## 时间介词

| 介词 | 用法 | 示例 |
|------|------|------|
| at | 精确时间点 | at 3 o'clock, at noon, at night |
| on | 具体日期/星期 | on Monday, on June 1st, on Christmas Day |
| in | 较长时间段 | in 2024, in June, in the morning, in summer |
| by | 在…之前/截止 | by 5 p.m., by the end of this month |
| for | 持续一段时间 | for two hours, for a long time |
| since | 从某时起（至今） | since 2020, since last Monday |
| during | 在…期间 | during the summer, during the meeting |

> 特例：in the morning / in the evening，但 at night。

## 地点介词

| 介词 | 用法 | 示例 |
|------|------|------|
| at | 小地点/具体位置 | at the bus stop, at home, at school |
| in | 大地点/空间内部 | in Beijing, in the room, in the box |
| on | 表面上 | on the table, on the wall, on the floor |

### 方向介词

| 介词 | 含义 | 示例 |
|------|------|------|
| to | 到、向 | go to school, come to me |
| into | 进入（内部） | walk into the room |
| onto | 到…上面 | jump onto the table |
| from | 从、来自 | come from China |
| through | 穿过（内部） | walk through the tunnel |
| across | 穿过（表面） | walk across the street |
| along | 沿着 | walk along the river |

## 方式介词

| 介词 | 用法 | 示例 |
|------|------|------|
| by | 交通方式（无冠词） | by bus, by plane, by email |
| in | 用语言/颜色 | in English, in red |
| with | 用工具/伴随 | write with a pen, with a friend |

## 常见介词搭配

### 形容词 + 介词

| 搭配 | 示例 |
|------|------|
| good at | She is good at math. |
| interested in | I'm interested in art. |
| afraid of | He is afraid of dogs. |
| responsible for | Who is responsible for this? |
| different from | This is different from that. |
| familiar with | Are you familiar with this? |

### 动词 + 介词

| 搭配 | 示例 |
|------|------|
| look at | Look at the blackboard. |
| listen to | Listen to the music. |
| wait for | Wait for me. |
| depend on | It depends on the weather. |
| look for | I'm looking for my keys. |
| think about | Think about your future. |

## 易混介词对比

| 对比 | 区别 | 示例 |
|------|------|------|
| in / on / at | in 大 → on 面 → at 点 | in China / on the desk / at the door |
| between / among | between 两者间；among 三者及以上 | between you and me / among the students |
| beside / besides | beside 在旁边；besides 除…之外（包含） | sit beside her / besides English |
| except / besides | except 除…外（不包含）；besides 除…外（包含） | everyone except me / besides math |
